Cameron Getner

DFT Simulations of Moiré Superlattices: Quantum Phase Transitions

Using Density Functional Theory to simulate electrons in moiré superlattices, we observe exotic quantum behaviors. Our research investigates two competing phases—trimer and honeycomb—and explores how an emergent Dirac cone in the band structure may drive transitions between these phases.
